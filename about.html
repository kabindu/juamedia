What Jua Media is

A digital marketing agency in Nairobi. Businesses hire us to run the marketing they do not have the time, the people or the tools to run themselves — and to show them what it returned.

We are the marketing department you do not have to hire

Most businesses we meet know their marketing could be better. What stops them is not ambition. It is that doing it properly means hiring a designer, a writer, someone who understands Google Ads, and someone who can build a website — and then managing all four.

We are that team, shared. You brief us once a month. We run your social accounts, your search advertising, your website and your content, and on the fifth of every month you get one report showing what we spent, how many enquiries it produced, what each one cost, and what we are changing next.

There is nothing to install and no system for your staff to learn. We are an agency, not a software company.

Three clients and a borrowed printer

We started in 2014 in the Nairobi CBD with a hardware shop, a driving school and a tour operator. All three are still with us.

At the time Kenyan businesses were being sold social media packages that meant three posts a week and a report full of likes. We took the view that marketing should be measured the way the rest of the business is measured: in enquiries, orders and shillings. Twelve years later that is still the whole method.

Jua is Kiswahili for sun, and kujua means to know. Good marketing does both: it puts a light on your business, and it teaches you what your customers actually want.

Four things we will not bend on

  • Measured, not assumedIf a campaign cannot be tracked to an enquiry, we say so before you pay for it.
  • You own everythingDomain, hosting, website and ad accounts are registered to your business, not ours.
  • Plain languageIf we cannot explain a tactic to your accountant in two sentences, it is probably not worth doing.
  • Start small, prove itBetter to win one channel on a modest budget than spread a small budget across five.

The short list

  • Take a percentage of your advertising spend — that would reward us for spending more of your money.
  • Guarantee rankings, revenue or a position on page one. Nobody can, and anyone who does is selling something else.
  • Report likes and impressions as results when you asked for customers.
  • Lock you into a long contract, or hold your accounts hostage if you leave.
  • Take on work we do not think we can improve. We will tell you and suggest what would actually help.
