A growing share of buyers now ask an assistant before they ask a search engine. They read a paragraph and act on it, often without visiting a website at all. If you are not named in that paragraph, you were never in the running, and no ranking report will tell you.

This is a new discipline but not a mysterious one. Assistants favour businesses that are described consistently, answer specific questions in writing, and are corroborated somewhere other than their own site.

Consistency beats sophistication

The brands that feel established here are rarely the most sophisticated. They are the ones that look identical everywhere: same name, same colour, same typeface, on the shopfront and the invoice alike.

A simple identity applied consistently reads as a serious business. A sophisticated one applied unevenly reads as several small ones.
