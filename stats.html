Social media

Where attention actually sits, and what it takes to hold it.

Social media

Short video out-reaches every other post format, by a wide margin

Across the accounts we run, a vertical video under 30 seconds reaches roughly four times as many people as a photo post on the same page.
Social media

Posting four to five times a week is the point of diminishing returns

Below three posts a week, reach falls away. Above five, the extra posts mostly reach the same people again.
Social media

68% of first contact now happens in the comments or DMs

Not on the website, and not on the phone. If nobody is watching the inbox, the enquiry is simply lost.
Social media

TikTok reaches the under-30 buyer at a fraction of the cost

For the same shillings, a TikTok campaign reaches far more 18 to 29 year olds than the equivalent spend on Facebook.
Social media

Replying within an hour roughly doubles the chance of a sale

The enquiry that waits until tomorrow has usually already messaged two of your competitors.
Social media

Nine in ten of the accounts we audit have no tracking on social clicks

They can tell you how many followers they gained. They cannot tell you which post brought a paying customer.

Search & AI answers

Google is no longer the only place a buyer asks a question.

Search & AI answers

A quarter of the searches we track now show an AI answer first

The buyer reads a summary before they ever see a list of websites. If you are not quoted in it, you are not in the running.
Search & AI answers

Being named by an AI assistant is rising faster than any ranking

Mentions of our clients inside ChatGPT, Gemini and AI Overviews have climbed steadily over the last six quarters.
Search & AI answers

Position one still takes about ten times the clicks of position ten

The gap between the top of page one and the bottom has not narrowed. It has widened.
Search & AI answers

Four in five local searches here end in a call, not a click

The map pack, the phone number and the opening hours do more work than the homepage.
Search & AI answers

Most sites we audit take over four seconds to show anything

Google counts that against you, and so does the person waiting on a Nairobi mobile connection.
Search & AI answers

A page that answers one question beats a page that lists ten services

Specific pages earn the AI citation. Catch-all pages get skipped by both the assistant and the buyer.

Ad spend & budgets

Where the money goes, and where it quietly leaks away.

Ad spend & budgets

Search ad costs have climbed every year we have measured them

The same click costs noticeably more than it did three years ago, which makes a wasted click more expensive too.
Ad spend & budgets

Roughly a third of a typical Google Ads budget is spent on the wrong search

Job hunters, students, people comparing prices in another country. All of it billable, none of it buying.
Ad spend & budgets

Retargeting is consistently the cheapest enquiry on the account

Showing an ad to someone who already visited costs a fraction of finding a stranger.
Ad spend & budgets

Budgets split across too many channels stop working

Three channels run properly beat seven channels run thinly, every time we have tested it.
Ad spend & budgets

Most accounts we inherit have never had a negative keyword added

It is the single fastest saving available, and it is free to do.
Ad spend & budgets

Ad fraud and bot clicks take a real bite out of display budgets

Display and programmatic are where it concentrates. Search and paid social are far cleaner.

Websites & conversion

Traffic you already pay for, and how much of it you are losing.

Websites & conversion

Four in five visits arrive on a phone

If the site was designed on a laptop and checked on a laptop, it was checked for the wrong person.
Websites & conversion

One second of extra load time costs about 7% of enquiries

The cost is invisible, because the people who leave never appear in your inbox to complain.
Websites & conversion

Shorter forms are answered far more often

Every extra field is another reason to give up. Three fields is usually enough to start a conversation.
Websites & conversion

A visible phone number lifts enquiries on nearly every site we test

Especially here, where a buyer would often rather call than type.
Websites & conversion

Most small business sites we audit have no working enquiry tracking

The form sends an email to an inbox nobody monitors, and nothing is recorded anywhere.
Websites & conversion

Pages that load fast keep people reading

Time on page roughly doubles once a site drops below two seconds to first paint.

WhatsApp & messaging

The channel most businesses here already use, and almost none measure.

WhatsApp & messaging

WhatsApp is the first choice for contact in most of our client accounts

Ahead of the phone call, the contact form and email combined.
WhatsApp & messaging

Messages are opened far more reliably than marketing email

The difference is not small. It is the difference between being read and being filed.
WhatsApp & messaging

A catalogue in the chat shortens the sale

Buyers who can see prices without leaving the conversation ask fewer questions and decide sooner.
WhatsApp & messaging

Reply speed matters more here than anywhere else

People expect a message back in minutes, because that is how they use the app with everyone else.
WhatsApp & messaging

Almost nobody tracks which advert produced the WhatsApp message

So the channel that produces the most enquiries is the one that gets the least credit at budget time.
WhatsApp & messaging

A saved reply set cuts response time without losing the human tone

Five prepared answers cover most of what people ask before they buy.

Ecommerce & payments

Carts, checkouts, and the moments where orders quietly disappear.

Ecommerce & payments

Around seven in ten carts are abandoned before payment

Most of them for reasons you can fix in an afternoon: unclear delivery cost, or no M-Pesa option at the top.
Ecommerce & payments

Mobile money is the default, not the alternative

A checkout that puts card first and M-Pesa second is asking most buyers to work harder.
Ecommerce & payments

Delivery cost shown late is the most common reason people leave

Shown early, it rarely stops the sale. Shown at the last step, it regularly does.
Ecommerce & payments

A reminder message recovers a useful share of abandoned carts

One message, sent within the hour, on the channel they already use.
Ecommerce & payments

Product photos shot on a real phone outperform stock images

Buyers here are looking for proof the item exists and is actually in Nairobi.
Ecommerce & payments

Repeat buyers cost almost nothing to reach

And they spend more. Most of the budget still goes to finding strangers.
