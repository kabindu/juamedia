Does this sound familiar?

You are probably here because of one of these

  • Your report opens with reach and mentions enquiries on page six.
  • Nobody can say which advert produced last month's best customer.
  • The form sends an email and nothing is recorded anywhere.
  • WhatsApp brings most of your enquiries and gets none of the credit.

You need four numbers: enquiries, cost per enquiry, source, and closed business. Everything else is context for those four.

If you can produce them every month you can run a marketing budget sensibly. If you cannot, you are guessing confidently.

How it works

If the report needs explaining, it is homework

A dashboard that takes effort to read does not get read. Ours fits on one screen and anyone in the business can glance at it and know whether the month is going well.

The monthly page is three minutes of reading and gives a director enough to ask a good question. Anything longer gets filed unread, which is the same as no report at all.
