Does this sound familiar?

You are probably here because of one of these

  • You sponsored something and have no idea whether it worked.
  • The radio spot ran and nothing was measured.
  • Your vehicle livery is unreadable from more than three metres.
  • The activation happened and nobody filmed any of it.

Offline media still moves product here, particularly outside the main cities and particularly for products people buy off a shelf. The problem is almost never the channel. It is that nobody measures it.

We plan offline the way we plan everything else: with a way to tell afterwards whether it worked.

How it works

Everything gets a way to be measured

A dedicated phone number on the billboard. A code read out on the radio. A short landing page for the activation. None of it is complicated and almost nobody does it.
