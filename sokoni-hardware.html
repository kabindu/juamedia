Google Ads · SEO

From wasted clicks to a full enquiry book

Same budget, rebuilt around the forty keywords that actually sell.

  • ClientSokoni Hardware

  • WhereNairobi CBD
  • ServicesGoogle Ads, SEO, conversion tracking
  • Timeframe9 months

The situation

Sokoni had been advertising on Google for two years and could not say whether it worked. The account spent a steady amount every month, the clicks came in, and the counter staff had no idea which customers had arrived that way.

When we read the search terms report for the first time, roughly a third of the spend was going to searches that could never buy: job seekers, students researching coursework, and shoppers in other countries comparing prices.

What we did

  1. Added conversion tracking for forms, calls and WhatsApp before changing anything, so there was a baseline to judge against.
  2. Built a negative keyword list from twelve months of search terms, which removed the job and coursework traffic in week one.
  3. Collapsed six thin campaigns into two with enough budget to learn from.
  4. Wrote separate pages for the four product lines that actually sell, and pointed each ad group at its own page.
  5. Set location targeting to presence only, so the budget stayed in the counties they deliver to.

What happened

Enquiries more than quadrupled on a budget that did not change. Cost per enquiry fell by just over two thirds, because the same money was finally reaching people who wanted to buy building supplies.

The more useful outcome was less dramatic: the counter team can now tell the owner which products the adverts are producing demand for, so stock decisions and advertising decisions are made in the same conversation.

Three months in we were paying less and getting three times the enquiries.
WK Wanjiru KamauOwner, Sokoni Hardware

What we would do next

The next step is their own product pages for the slower-moving lines, so the SEO work can carry traffic the advertising currently pays for.
